# Launch Emails

Internal and external launch communications for FINN Remarketing Tech features.

## Naming convention

`{YYYY-MM-DD}-{feature-name}-launch.md`

Audience can be tagged in the file front matter (`audience: internal` / `audience: suppliers` / `audience: buyers`).

## What belongs here

- Internal launch announcements (Slack `#rem_tech_general`, broader FINN-wide announcements)
- Supplier-facing or buyer-facing rollout emails
- Stakeholder pre-briefs that go to leadership

For one-off marketing copy that doesn't accompany a feature launch, keep it in HubSpot/Mailchimp instead.
