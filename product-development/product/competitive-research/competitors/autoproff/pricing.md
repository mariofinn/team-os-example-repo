# AUTOproff — Pricing

**Last reviewed:** 2026-05-08

---

## What's known

- **Subscription / flat-rate model** per dealership, with unlimited users and access to all platform functions. This contrasts sharply with the per-transaction models of BCA (3% auction fee) and CarOnSale (€119 flat per sale).
- **Reference data point (Oct 2020):** €1,000 / dealership flat-rate, all-in. Treat as historical — current rates almost certainly differ and vary by market.
- **Pricing is region-specific.** Each market (DE, NL, AT, etc.) has its own pricing. The public [autoproff.com/prices](https://www.autoproff.com/prices) page is a portal that routes to local sites or to "contact sales".
- **Integrated services** (on-site appraisal, transport, escrow, guaranteed minimum prices) are part of the platform offering — not separately metered in public materials. Likely either bundled into the subscription or marketed as on-demand premium services.

## Notes for FINN

- **Subscription pricing is structurally different** from the rest of the field. When suppliers / dealers compare AUTOproff vs. CarOnSale vs. BCA, it's apples-to-oranges. Easy place to lose a price-comparison conversation if FINN doesn't reframe to net-cost-per-sold-car.
- **AS24 distribution lift** justifies a price premium for dealer-buyers — they get reach. FINN doesn't have AS24's consumer-side reach, but for B2B-only flows (dealer-to-dealer trade) the FINN buyer network can compete on density within DACH if scaled deliberately.
