# Auto1 — Pricing

**Source:** [Auto1 price list 2026-01-01 (PDF)](https://content.auto1.com/static/car_images/price_list_de_2026-01-01.pdf)
**Last reviewed:** 2026-05-08

---

## Seller-side fees

| Item | Fee |
|------|-----|
| Mobile evaluation (Auto1 evaluator visits dealer) | €29 |
| Logistics — pick-up by Auto1 | €149 |
| Logistics — buyer delivers to Auto1 location | €29 |
| Logistics — buyer delivers to Auto1 reconditioning centre | €0 |

## Buyer-side fees

| Item | Fee |
|------|-----|
| Car handling | €289 |
| Standing fee (if pick-up delayed) | €15 / day |
| Document handling — domestic | €119 |
| Document handling — international | €159 |
| Transport | Variable |
| Handling for second set of tyres | €29 |
| Auction fee | €0–€1,950 (tiered, embedded in auction) |

## Notes for FINN

- Seller economics look attractive on the surface (€29 evaluation), but the auction fee on the buyer side eats into gross sale price the supplier sees. For supplier-volume modelling, the **net to seller** matters more than the headline seller fee.
- The €0 option for buyer-delivery to a reconditioning centre is a deliberate funnel into Auto1's in-house refurbishment pipeline.
