# Autorola — TL;DR

**Last reviewed:** 2026-05-08

---

## What they do

Pan-European online vehicle remarketing operator selling **white-label** auction infrastructure to OEMs and large fleets, plus operating its own MarketPlace. Very strong in OEM-controlled remarketing — their flagship reference is a multi-year **pan-European contract with the BMW Group** covering BMW national sales companies, Alphabet Fleet Management, and BMW Financial Services.

The product suite has three named offerings:
- **MarketPlace** — the remarketing auction platform
- **INDICATA** — real-time used-car pricing data product
- **Fleet Monitor** — asset management platform for fleet operators

## Focus segments

| Supplier archetype | Fit |
|--------------------|-----|
| OEMs (RaaS / white-label) | **Highest** — their core ICP, demonstrated with BMW Group |
| Banks / leasing | High — works inside fleet contracts (e.g., Alphabet) |
| Dealers | Medium — they run open auctions but dealer-flow isn't the headline |

## Geographies

**~17 European countries** per their FAQ. Multi-language. Pan-European footprint is a stated competitive advantage when pitching OEMs.

## Scale

- ~70,000 active buyers on the platform
- ~200,000 vehicles per year listed
- 24-hour auction format

## Differentiators

- **White-label auctions:** Closed, branded auctions running on Autorola infrastructure — exactly the model OEMs want when they need brand control and dealer-channel routing. This is the most direct head-to-head with FINN's RaaS pitch.
- **Auction format flexibility:** Open, closed, buy-now, and white-label can all run on the same platform across countries and languages.
- **INDICATA pricing data:** Independent, real-time used-car pricing as a standalone product — useful even when the customer doesn't auction with them.
- **Fleet Monitor:** Asset-management software layer above the auction; sticky for large fleet operators.
- **Long-term OEM contracts:** BMW signed a four-year pan-European deal (Oct 2022, renewed Nov 2023). Multi-year contracts create switching-cost moats.

## Strengths to be aware of

- The most direct competitor for any OEM RaaS conversation. If an OEM mentions "we're talking to Autorola", treat it as serious.
- BMW pan-EU win is a compelling reference for any OEM buyer evaluating white-label models.
- INDICATA is a genuine product moat, not just a service add-on.
- 17-country footprint matches the pan-EU footprint OEMs increasingly demand.

## Weaknesses / opportunities for FINN

- They are **infrastructure-first**: they sell the platform, but the supplier still owns operational responsibility. FINN's RaaS includes the operational layer (compound, transport, refurb, document handling) — this is a meaningfully different and often more compelling pitch for OEMs with lean teams (validated in supplier discovery: Christoph, Stefan).
- White-label deployments take long sales cycles. If FINN can land an OEM with a faster, fuller-stack offering, it sidesteps Autorola's incumbent-platform advantage.
- DACH is where FINN can move fastest. Autorola is pan-European but not necessarily DACH-dominant.
