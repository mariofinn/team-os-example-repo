## What they do

B2B used-car auction platform aimed primarily at **dealer-to-dealer** trade. Strong in dealer trade-in flows, with on-site appraisal services, escrow payments, and buyer financing built in. Less of a fleet/OEM RaaS play than Auto1 or BCA, but invests heavily in dealer-side experience.

## Focus segments

| Supplier archetype | Fit |
|--------------------|-----|
| OEMs (RaaS) | Low — not their primary segment |
| Banks / leasing | Medium — capable but not dominant |
| Dealers | Highest — ICP; their entire product surface targets dealer trade-ins |

## Geographies

DACH-anchored, expanding across Europe.

## Differentiators

- **Free on-site appraisal:** A CarOnSale expert visits the dealer's lot, records condition, generates a comprehensive condition report and auction listing per vehicle. No fee.
- **Market Price Inquiry tool:** Computes current market price within seconds using their own transaction database + expert overlay.
- **COSPay escrow:** Buyer pays into COS escrow → seller is notified to release the car → COS guarantees payment. Removes buyer credit risk and shortens the seller's cash-conversion cycle (no bank-clearance wait).
- **Buy Now, Pay Later for buyers:** Dealer-buyers can defer payment by 30–90 days; seller is paid immediately. Increases buyer participation and price ceilings.
- **Guaranteed Price for trade-ins:** Minimum price guarantee on the dealer's trade-in vehicle, eliminating appraisal risk for the dealer.
- **Dedicated account manager** from day one — handles all buyer questions, negotiations, complaints in the background.
- **Refurbishment as a seller decision pre-auction** (rather than embedded in flow).

## Pricing model

Pure success fee: **€119 flat per vehicle sold**, no base fee, no listing fees. Full detail in [`pricing.md`](pricing.md).

## Strengths to be aware of

- The €119 flat fee is a powerful pitch for dealer trade-ins — hard to beat on simplicity
- COSPay + BNPL stack is a coherent, modern dealer-buyer experience
- Dedicated-account-manager service tier is genuinely high-touch

## Weaknesses / opportunities for FINN

- They're not built for OEM RaaS — different ICP; FINN is largely operating in a different lane on the supplier side.
- Where overlap exists (dealer trade-in flow inside the buyer product), FINN needs to match COS's simplicity on fees and process to compete for trade-in volume.
