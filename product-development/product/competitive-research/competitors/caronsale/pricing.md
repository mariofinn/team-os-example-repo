# CarOnSale — Pricing

**Source:** [caronsale.com](https://www.caronsale.com/en)
**Last reviewed:** 2026-05-08

---

## Seller-side fees

| Item | Fee |
|------|-----|
| Listing | €0 |
| On-site appraisal | €0 |
| Success fee (per car sold) | **€119 flat** |

No base fee; suppliers only pay on success. This is the clearest "no risk to try us" pitch in the segment.

## Buyer-side

- Buy Now, Pay Later: 30–90 day deferral available to dealer-buyers; seller is paid immediately (CarOnSale carries the float).
- Guaranteed Price service for trade-ins (minimum-price guarantee).

Specific buyer-side fees not published in detail in source. Confirm if needed by walking through a live listing.

## Notes for FINN

- A flat €119 success fee is *very* aggressive vs. percentage-based auction fees (BCA's 3% / min €450 → much higher absolute on most vehicles). For a €15,000 sale: BCA buyer pays €450, CarOnSale seller pays €119. Different sides of the trade, but the headline is striking.
- The flat-fee structure is what dealers remember. If FINN's buyer product introduces percentage fees, expect comparison pushback.
