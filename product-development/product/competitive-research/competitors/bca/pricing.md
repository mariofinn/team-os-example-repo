# BCA — Pricing

**Source:** [bca.com — Payment and Fees](https://www.bca.com/de_DE/Payment-and-Fees)
**Last reviewed:** 2026-05-08

---

## Seller-side fees

**None.** All revenue comes from the buyer side.

## Buyer-side fees

| Item | Fee |
|------|-----|
| Auction fee | 3% (min €450) — Nissan negotiated rate: **2.62%** |
| Export fee | €140 |
| Standing fee | €18 / day (first 7 working days free) |
| Collection — domestic | €15 |
| Collection — international | €40 |
| Transport | Variable |
| Photos | €29 |
| Battery check | €40 |

## Buyer conditions

- Payment due within 2 days of invoicing
- If buyers don't want delivery, they actively have to opt out — see [BCA After-Sales Transport](https://www.bca.com/de/de/Hauptmenue/After-sales/COLUMN1/transport/#Drittanbieter-Abholung)

## Notes for FINN

- BCA's "free for sellers" framing is powerful in supplier conversations — but the buyer-side auction fee compresses the price the seller realises. Compare net-to-seller, not headline.
- The 2.62% Nissan rate is a real data point on what large OEM contracts negotiate down to. Useful as an anchor for FINN's commercial conversations with OEM prospects.
- 7-free-days standing fee is a generous lever for dealer buyers — FINN's compound dwell-time targets need to compete on overall calendar time, not just days at compound.
