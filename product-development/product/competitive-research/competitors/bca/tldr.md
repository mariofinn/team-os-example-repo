# BCA — TL;DR

**Slug:** `bca`
**Website:** [bca.com](https://www.bca.com/de_DE/) · [bca-remarketing.de](https://www.bca-remarketing.de/)
**HQ:** Pan-European group (UK origin, large DE presence)
**Last reviewed:** 2026-05-08

---

## What they do

Long-established B2B used-car auction operator. Runs the dominant European auction marketplace, plus full-service remarketing operations: physical compounds, transport fleet, appraisal, refurbishment, and instant-buyout programmes. The deep operational stack (own transport, own logistics centres, in-house reconditioning) is the core moat.

## Focus segments

| Supplier archetype | Fit |
|--------------------|-----|
| OEMs (RaaS) | High — historically the default OEM remarketing partner in DE; OEMs view BCA as the trusted incumbent |
| Banks / leasing | High — proven bulk processing |
| Dealers | High — the BCA Ankauf flow is squarely a dealer trade-in tool |

## Geographies

Pan-European, very strong in Germany. Multiple DE logistics centres and own-fleet transport across Europe.

## Differentiators

- **Marktpreis 2.0:** Pricing tool drawing on thousands of European transaction data points + expert checks. Daily market price computed within minutes.
- **VIN-based upload + email-list ingest:** Low-friction supplier onboarding (no app required).
- **Four tiers of refurbishment** (interior + exterior preparation packages).
- **Own transport fleet across Europe** + multiple DE logistics centres — they don't depend on third-party logistics.
- **BCA Ankauf:** Instant buyout for dealer trade-ins. Price determined via app in ~10 minutes, payment within 3 days. Free pickup. Removes appraisal risk and unsold-car risk for the dealer.
- **BCA Ankauf Plus:** If BCA later sells the car for more than the buyout price, **100% of the upside is returned** to the original seller as a bonus. Unusual structure — captures the dealer's upside while BCA monetises the float and process.
- **Single counterparty for risk:** BCA collects from buyers, manages claims, handles after-sale issues — supplier sees one party.
- **Closed-network capability:** Can run brand-restricted dealer auctions (used heavily for OEM franchise channels).

## Pricing model

**Seller fees: none.** All revenue comes from buyer-side fees. Auction fee is 3% (min €450), with negotiated rates for large suppliers (Nissan: 2.62%). Full breakdown in [`pricing.md`](pricing.md).

## Strengths to be aware of

- Trust-as-a-moat with OEM remarketing leaders — they "just know BCA"
- Vertical integration is hard to match operationally without huge capex
- Ankauf Plus is a clever profit-share structure that creates dealer stickiness

## Weaknesses / opportunities for FINN

- **Nissan switched away from BCA to DEKRA in 2024** after a tender. Christoph's stated reasons: professionalism, reporting quality, speed, process stability — *not* price. Implication: BCA's incumbency is not unbeatable; service quality and proactivity win.
- Renault's Kirsten describes BCA's FCM pricing automation as broken for 3+ years (workaround: she submits minimum prices manually monthly). BCA has not invested in fixing it because other markets take priority. This is a real product gap FINN could solve.
- BCA emails / Excel-based supplier comms scale poorly. Supplier discovery flagged this as the #1 cross-supplier pain point.
- Old auction system experience ("System landscape from the 80s, not sexy" — Christoph) leaves room for a modern UX to differentiate.
