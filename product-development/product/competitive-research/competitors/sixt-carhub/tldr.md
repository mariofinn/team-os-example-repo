# SIXT CarHub — TL;DR

**Slug:** `sixt-carhub`
**Website:** [sixtcarhub.com](https://www.sixtcarhub.com/)

**Launched:** November 2025
**Last reviewed:** 2026-05-08

---

## What they do

Brand-new digital remarketing platform spun out of SIXT's internal used-car operations. Originally built to sell SIXT's own ex-rental fleet, the platform was opened to **external** fleet operators in November 2025 — leasing companies, banks, insurers, and OEMs. The pitch is a fully comprehensive **consignment** structure: seller retains ownership of the vehicle while SIXT runs marketing, the sales process, and transaction management end-to-end.

## Focus segments

| Supplier archetype | Fit |
|--------------------|-----|
| OEMs (RaaS) | High — explicitly named as a target customer |
| Banks / leasing / insurers | **Highest** — primary stated ICP |
| Dealers | Low — not their lane |

This is the most directly model-aligned competitor to FINN's RaaS pitch on the supplier side.

## Geographies

**Initial rollout:** Germany, Austria, Italy, France, Spain.

## Differentiators

- **Consignment model with retained ownership:** SIXT markets and sells but the vehicle stays on the seller's books until sale. SIXT calls this "the first fully comprehensive consignment structure" in the segment. This is structurally identical to what FINN positions as RaaS.
- **Asset-light for the seller:** Upload, then SIXT handles technical inspection, price proposal, transaction management. The seller's operational burden is minimal.
- **SIXT consignment dealer network:** Sales go through a verified network of dealers who sell to end customers — i.e., the buyer base is curated, not open-marketplace.
- **SIXT brand and operational track record:** Years of in-house remarketing on their own fleet → credibility on operational quality.
- **Multi-country day-one:** 5 EU markets at launch is fast for a Nov-2025 product.

## Pricing model

Not publicly published. Expect bespoke deal economics for fleet operators given the consignment structure (SIXT carries float / process cost; takes a margin or fee per sale).

## Strengths to be aware of

- **Direct model match.** Anyone evaluating FINN's RaaS will likely also evaluate SIXT CarHub. We will be compared head-to-head.
- **Brand trust.** SIXT is a recognised European mobility brand — easier to get into procurement conversations than a startup.
- **Consignment dealer network already in place.** For a consignment model, the buyer-network density is the limiting factor. SIXT brought one with them.
- **5-country launch** signals serious ambition and capital backing.

## Weaknesses / opportunities for FINN

- **Brand-new product externally** (Nov 2025). The first 12 months are typically rocky on integrations, tooling, and supplier UX. FINN can win pilots based on speed-to-stand-up and tighter supplier feedback loops.
- **Buyer network is curated, not open.** Constrains price discovery vs. open-marketplace models — relevant for suppliers who care about price ceiling more than channel control.
- **SIXT's incentive alignment.** Suppliers may worry SIXT's own fleet vehicles get priority routing or pricing; transparency on this is going to be a recurring objection FINN doesn't have.
- **No deep operational stack disclosed yet** (compound, refurb). They handle "inspection and price proposal" — unclear how much of the physical de-fleeting flow they own vs. outsource.
