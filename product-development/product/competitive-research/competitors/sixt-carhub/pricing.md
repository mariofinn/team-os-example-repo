# SIXT CarHub — Pricing

**Last reviewed:** 2026-05-08

---

## What's known

- Consignment model: seller retains ownership; SIXT runs marketing + sales + transaction management → SIXT's revenue likely comes from a per-sale fee or margin spread.
- No public price list as of May 2026 (product is ~6 months old externally).

## What's not known

| Question | Status |
|----------|--------|
| Per-vehicle fee or margin structure | Not published |
| Whether supplier pays a base / SaaS fee or pure success fee | Not published |
| Pricing differentiation by supplier archetype (OEM vs. leasing vs. insurer) | Not known |
| Pricing for SIXT's own ex-fleet vehicles vs. external suppliers (potential conflict-of-interest signal) | Not known |

## Notes for FINN

- When competing for a deal, ask the prospect what SIXT quoted (or expect to quote). The opacity is itself useful intel.
- If SIXT's pricing emerges as "% of sale price," compare on net-to-seller. If it's "fixed per car," compare on dealer-network density and price-discovery breadth.
- The SIXT-fleet conflict-of-interest is a real concern for OEMs — emphasise FINN's neutrality (no own fleet to prioritise).
