## 1 – Pillars of Supplier Value

**Liquidity Velocity:** Speed of converting a physical asset into working capital. Every day a vehicle sits in a compound it incurs holding costs (depreciation, storage, cost of capital).

**Price Maximization:** Ability to find the highest payer regardless of geography. Often involves cross-border arbitrage, leveraging 40,000+ buyers per platform.

**Operational Friction:** Labor and cost required to upload a vehicle, sell it, and handle post-sale operations.

**Risk Transfer:** Degree to which the platform shields the supplier from buyer default, mechanical disputes, and regulatory complexities (VAT, export).

## 2 – Supplier Archetypes and Needs Analysis

**OEMs**
- Volume: High, but variable (Buybacks, Employee cars)
- Core Need: Brand protection, closed networks, residual value (RV) management
- Key Competitors: BCA, Autorola, Auto1
- **Primary research (Nissan, Renault — Jan/Feb 2026):**
  - Dealer channel protection is real for Renault (90% franchise routing, JGW incentive system) but a soft preference for Nissan (no GW quotas, "jeder darf kaufen solang der Preis stimmt")
  - Pricing is the primary volume blocker, not availability or process
  - OEMs chose their service partner (DEKRA over BCA) based on proactivity, reporting quality, and reliability — not primarily price
  - All operations are outsourced to compound operators (DEKRA, BLG) — any platform must integrate into this, not bypass it
  - Manual Excel/email process is accepted today but won't scale; OEMs are open to a shared digital workspace
  - Market pricing data (Mobile.de aggregates) is a differentiated value-add Nissan explicitly requested

**Banks**
- Volume: High (thousands of units/month)
- Core Need: Process stability, bulk logistics, service capacity, RV optimization, guaranteed capacity
- Key Competitors: Auto1, Autorola, SixtCarHub

**Dealerships**
- Volume: Fragmented (Trade-ins)
- Core Need: Risk mitigation (pricing uncertainty), speed of settlement, ease of use (mobile apps)
- Key Competitors: CarOnSale, Auto1 Group

## 4 – Digital Offerings

### Vehicle Upload & Integration Tools
- **AUTO1:** Self-service evaluation app (15 min), API integration for bulk partners
- **CarOnSale:** Dealer system integration, seamless data transfer with minimal manual effort
- **BCA:** VIN-based upload or list sharing via email

### Pricing Tools
- **AUTO1:** "Price Indicator" — instant market-based price estimates for trade-ins
- **CarOnSale:** "Market Price Inquiry" — uses transaction database + expert knowledge, computes current market price within seconds
- **BCA:** "Marktpreis 2.0" — thousands of European transaction data points + expert checks, daily market price within minutes

### Digital Sales Management
- **AUTO1:** Remarketing Dashboard with real-time auction monitoring (bids, buyer interest, status), report downloads, counter-offer negotiation tool (anonymous counter-offer to highest bidder below minimum price)
- **CarOnSale:** Full auction visibility + COSPay escrow system — seller notified immediately when buyer pays, car released before bank clearance. COS guarantees payment to seller, removing buyer credit risk.

### Closed Networks
Allows suppliers to sell to a closed loop (e.g., own brand dealers) before opening to the public. Most relevant for OEMs.

## 5 – Service Offerings

### Appraisal & Listing Service
- **AUTO1:** Evaluation app or on-site mobile evaluators at the dealership
- **CarOnSale:** Free on-site appraisers — visits dealer's lot, records details, generates condition report and auction listing

### Cleaning & Reconditioning
- **BCA:** 4 tiers of interior/exterior preparation
- **Auto1:** Extensive reconditioning and cleaning, production centers outside Germany
- **CarOnSale:** Refurbishment as a seller decision before auction

### Logistics & Transportation
- **BCA:** Own transport fleet across Europe, multiple logistics centers in Germany
- **CarOnSale:** Arranges vehicle collection and delivery as part of one-stop service
- **AUTO1:** Partners with logistics providers for tailored transport solutions

### Guaranteed Purchase & Trade-In Programs
- **BCA "Ankauf":** Instant buyout at fixed price (determined via app in ~10 min), payment within 3 days. "Ankauf Plus": if BCA sells higher than buyout price, 100% of extra profit returned to original seller.
- **CarOnSale:** "Guaranteed Price" — minimum price guarantee for trade-in vehicles

### Single Contractual Partner & Risk Mitigation
- **AUTO1:** Acts as sole contractual buyer after auction — pays seller directly, handles buyer collection
- **CarOnSale:** Personal account manager handles all buyer questions, negotiations, complaints in the background
- **BCA:** Takes over payment collection, manages after-sale issues via claims department

## 5 – Commercial Model

**Success-Based Fee:** No upfront listing fees; commission or flat fee charged only on sale.
- **CarOnSale:** No base fee, flat €119 per vehicle sold
- **Autorola:** Success-fee model

**Personalized Support & Expertise:**
- **CarOnSale:** Dedicated account manager from day one, coordinates everything
- **AUTO1:** Industry expert advisors reachable by phone/email
- **SIXT CarHub:** Markets as trusted B2B partner, advises fleet operators; auto-optimizes channel selection (dealer network vs. export vs. retail)

---

## 6 – Buyer Side (Impact on Seller)

### Buyer Financing & Secure Payment
- **CarOnSale:** "Buy Now, Pay Later" — buyer pays 30–90 days later, supplier paid immediately. Increases buyer participation and purchasing power.
- **AUTO1:** Integrated dealer financing via Auto1 FT unit; acts as sole contract partner, pays seller directly after auction, handles buyer collection.

## 7 – Pricing

### Auto1
**Seller:**
- Mobile Evaluation: €29
- Logistics pick-up by Auto1: €149
- Delivery by buyer to Auto1 location: €29
- Delivery by buyer to Auto1 center: €0

**Buyer:**
- Car Handling: €289
- Standing fee (if pick-up delayed): €15/day
- Document Handling: €119 (domestic) / €159 (international)
- Transport: Variable
- Second set of tires: €29
- Auction Fee: €0–€1,950

### BCA
**Seller:** None

**Buyer:**
- Auction fee: 3%, min. €450 (Nissan: 2.62%)
- Export fee: €140
- Standing fee: €18 (7 working days free)
- Collection: €15 (domestic) / €40 (international)
- Transport: Variable
- Photos: €29
- Battery Check: €40
- Payment due within 2 days of invoicing
