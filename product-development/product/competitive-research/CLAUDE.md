## What to track per competitor

| Dimension | Why it matters |
|-----------|---------------|
| Model (buy-and-resell vs. consignment vs. white-label) | Determines whether they're structurally aligned or misaligned with OEM brand-protection asks |
| Geographies | DACH-only vs. pan-European changes the OEM pitch |
| Supplier ICP | Which archetype (OEM / bank / leasing / dealer) they actually win with — not what they claim |
| Pricing structure | Per-car flat fee vs. % vs. enterprise contract changes how suppliers compare net economics |
| Operational depth | Do they own compound / transport / refurb, or just the auction layer |
| Buyer-side mechanics | Financing, escrow, BNPL — drives price ceilings and seller cash velocity |
| Reference accounts | Public OEM/fleet wins create switching costs we have to overcome |

## When to update

- After competitive deal wins/losses (supplier or buyer side)
- When a competitor launches a notable feature
- After supplier or buyer calls where competitors are mentioned
- Quarterly review of pricing and positioning
- When primary research surfaces new competitor intel — update both per-competitor tldrs and the master overview
