---

#### Operational Model: DEKRA as General Contractor

Christoph is the sole FTE at Nissan handling used cars (Nadja Schmidt joined Feb 2026 as support). Because of this lean structure, Nissan outsources almost everything to DEKRA Automotive Solutions:

**DEKRA scope:** Sales (telemarketing, Lineos platform), marketing, administration, vehicle appraisals, registration/de-registration, document handling, logistics via Hapitec/CAT, invoicing and dunning.

**Implication:** Christoph is forced to "blindly trust" DEKRA — he physically lacks the time to be involved in operations. He is only involved when there are problems or when package price approvals are needed.

**Volume:** ~6,500 vehicles/year. Only possible with DEKRA's 3+ dedicated FTE plus back-office support.

**Key learning:** DEKRA's value proposition goes well beyond selling cars — it covers the entire defleeting process. This is what FINN needs to understand about what OEMs value in a service partner.

---

#### Why Nissan Chose DEKRA (and What This Signals for FINN)

Nissan was with BCA for 25 years before switching to DEKRA after a tender (DEKRA onboarded Aug 1, 2024). Competitors in the tender: BCA, Auto1, Autorola, TÜV Süd. Christoph's view: Auto1 is not a serious competitor in this market.

**Why DEKRA won (in Christoph's words, not procurement's):** Professionalism, reporting quality, speed, and process stability. Procurement cared about price; Christoph cared about service quality.

**DEKRA pricing model:** Fixed monthly fee for fleet management (the 3 FTE) + variable fee under €200 per vehicle sold. Comparison: BCA was 2.62% variable per car.

**What he values:** Proactivity — solving problems before he even hears about them. "Wenn jemand ein Problem sieht, das behiebt und das ist vielleicht nicht mal erwähnt." This is what gives him peace of mind.

---

#### Financial & Sales Structure

- Nissan sets target prices based on book/residual values. DEKRA has authority to negotiate lower (similar to FINN's approach).
- Package deal discounts require Christoph's manual approval via email/Excel. DEKRA sends a standard template with RV; Christoph approves by email.
- For very large deviations: Finance Director must sign off.
- Nissan has full transparency on achieved sale prices.
- Market context: he mentions a "negative trend" where achieving target prices is increasingly rare. Grey imports make his life difficult — a market factor no service provider can fix.

**Sales channels:**
- Telesales by 3 DEKRA FTE
- Lineos platform (Nissan's online remarketing platform, also outdated)

**Dealer channel:** Used cars have NO dealer quota obligations (only Tageszulassungen must go to franchise dealers). Christoph can sell to anyone who pays the best price. Nissan dealer network is the preferred channel but not protected.

---

#### Major Pain Points: Legacy Systems & Manual Process

**System landscape:** UVMS (Used Vehicle Management System) and Lineos are described as being from the 80s, "not sexy," Excel-based. Cannot handle 360° images or modern features. Starfleet (DEKRA's next-gen system) is blocked by Nissan Europa — Christoph's frustration is directed at European HQ, not at DEKRA.

**Communication:** Package negotiations happen via fragmented email chains and Excel templates. At low volumes this is manageable. At higher volumes, it becomes "unclear" and "unorganized" — Christoph is constantly searching for emails to check payment status, price approvals, and what is still open.

---

#### The "Live List" Solution (His Idea, Not Ours)

Christoph proposed the solution himself:

**Format:** Shared online list (SharePoint preferred — Nissan cannot use Google Sheets).

**Workflow:**
- DEKRA fills the list with ~50-60 available cars at a time, continuously replenished
- FINN marks interest, enters counter-offers, or flags cars as sold
- Christoph manages exceptions only (price deviations). Standard prices → DEKRA invoices automatically without his involvement
- DEKRA issues POAs based on the list

**Benefit:** Real-time transparency on blocked inventory. Christoph wants to know immediately if FINN is no longer interested in a reserved car so he can sell it elsewhere.

**Two separate lists proposed:** One for external (stock) vehicles, one for FINN fleet returns (pre-sold/preselling).

---

#### Pricing & Negotiation Logic

Christoph sets target prices from residual values. Discount criteria:
1. **Volume** — larger package = more flexibility
2. **Days on lot (Standtage)** — cars sitting 3-4 months get much more flexibility than fresh arrivals
3. **Model popularity** — hard-to-sell models get easier discounts

**Main bottleneck:** Supply is not the constraint. "I could give you 100 cars tomorrow. The problem is you probably can't sell them all... it's purely a pricing issue."

**Market data:** Christoph uses Autobiz (Neptune/Stellantis) but lacks time to analyze raw data. He explicitly asked for FINN's aggregated Mobile.de retail prices — "ready to use" market analysis would be a strong value-add. Said "Definitive" when Mario offered it.

---

#### Strategic Requirement: Regional Buyer Transparency

Christoph allows FINN to sell to anyone, but requires visibility on where cars go regionally. The risk he wants to avoid: a freelance dealer buying cheap Nissan vehicles via FINN and then undercutting a neighboring official Nissan dealer in Germany. He prefers sales that go to conflict-free regions or export — but legally permits all destinations. This is a transparency ask, not a hard block on who can buy.

---

#### Operational Details

**Appraisals:** Open to exploring shared appraisals for FINN fleet returns to save time/cost. Hurdle: different damage catalogs. Christoph suspects Nissan/DEKRA valuations may be lower (stricter) than FINN's — which could be advantageous for FINN.

**POA process:** Simple. DEKRA confirms payment → Admin team sends release email to Hapitec (compound/logistics partner) and FINN. Collection requires only this email and a 6-digit PIN.

- Tim Hartdegen is the primary operational contact. Christoph doesn't want to be in the day-to-day.
- He responds well to data. Share Mobile.de aggregate pricing proactively — easy win.
- Frame the live list / portal as a scaling tool, not a fix for a current crisis.
- Proactivity is the #1 relationship factor. Anticipate problems, don't make him ask twice.

#### Team Structure

#### Remarketing Channels

- **80% BCA auctions** (15-year partnership): Open auctions, Tuesdays and Thursdays at 10am (Live-Bid format). She deliberately keeps Live-Bid over X-Bid (BCA's automated online bidding) because it works better for her dealer network.
- **20% BCA telesales:** Two BCA staff send offers to dealers by email/phone. Refurbished vehicles (€500+ damage threshold) go here.
- **Small volume to Auto1:** Residual channel for cars that don't sell at BCA.
- **Third path** for anything that still doesn't move.

**Auction format:** Open (not dealer-exclusive). She achieves better prices with open market access. Closed auctions only for specials. 70-80% conversion rate in first auction.

---

#### The FCM System (Core Process Detail)

**What it is:** Fleet Control Monitor — shared system between Renault and BCA (BCA has a different "blue" interface on the same platform). Belongs to the BCA/Cox Automotive group. Renault Germany was the pilot country — they've had it for 3 years.

**What it does:** Vehicle lifecycle management — appraisals, photos (Beauty Shots), documents, lebenszyklus (lifecycle). BCA can access and feed data back into it.

**The workaround:** FCM has a price suggestion feature that "funktioniert noch nicht so rein." Because of this, Kirsten still sends minimum prices to BCA manually via Excel. This has been the workaround for 3 years. It creates time costs and error sources but hasn't been fixed because all other markets are now rolling out FCM and there's no manpower available.

**Data quality:** Fields like fuel type show "Kraftstoffart unbekannt" — data quality gaps come from France (Frankreich) not populating fields correctly. BCA has a workaround too. Kirsten calls it "son bisschen 'ne Blackbox."

---

#### Pricing Methodology

- Works entirely in residual value percentages, never in EUR directly
- Base: YTD historical performance as benchmark
- Age adjustment: **-1.5% RV per 3 months of age**
- Adjustments for km and damage
- Does a "Kontrollisation" — clean/normalized comparison price (as if vehicle had no damage) for comparability across vehicles
- DAT valuations available but rarely used ("costs €1-2 per query, rarely worth it")

**Reserve prices:** Set manually in Excel and sent to BCA — this is the workaround described above.

---

#### Financial Constraints (Hard Operational Requirements)

- **Month-end:** No open receivables allowed. No invoicing on the last day of the month. Finance team monitors payments before releasing vehicles.
- **Half-year & year-end targets:** Mandatory inventory reduction at end of June and December. These are fixed, non-negotiable clearance cycles.
- She has already written month-end restrictions into the FINN contract draft.
- Payment release process: DEKRA/finance confirms payment → Transportfreigabe (transport release) and document transfer. Payment must be confirmed on bank account before release — not just transferred.

---
