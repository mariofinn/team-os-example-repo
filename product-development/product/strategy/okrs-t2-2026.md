# OKRs - T2 2026

**Domain:** Remarketing
**Trimester:** T2 2026 (May - August 2026)
**Focus:** Remarketing as a Service (RAS) — all four KRs are RAS-focused

---

## Objective: Scale Remarketing as a Service into a profitable, reliable platform

---

### KR1: Grow the remarketing profit center (supply variety + volume)

Secure written commitments from multiple suppliers to ensure both volume and assortment variety.
- Stellantis (6-8 brands) — considered "rather safe," target close within T2
- Renault — "rather safe"
- MG — early stage
- Nissan — already active (~5,500 cars)
- Goal: 3+ committed suppliers with written commitments by end of T2

**Why:** 5,500 Nissan cars alone is not enough for meaningful scale. Need assortment variety alongside volume.

---

### KR2: Increase Monthly Active Users (MAU) on buyer portal

**Current:** ~230-300 MAU
**Levers:**
- Agentic sales: outreach to new dealers via Tracksites leads (Denmark), agent enriches leads and sends emails in remarketing manager's name
- Reactivate dormant dealers (>60 days inactive)
- Regular email campaigns to existing active dealers

---

### KR3: Reduce de-fleeting cycle time

**Current:** 63 days (last 30-day average)
**T2 Target:** 21 days
**Industry benchmark:** BCA/AutoOne 1-2 weeks; OEM direct 2-3 weeks
**Future target:** 14 days (beyond T2)

**Sub-steps being tracked:**
1. Ready to process → supply invoice issued (~30 days currently — "way too much")
2. Supply invoice sent to FINN (supplier-side delay, ~10 days, cannot control)
3. Supply invoice received → sales invoice issued (should be automated, tracking may be buggy)
4. Sales invoice issued → dealer pays (~7 day payment terms; best-in-class is 2-3 days)
5. Sales invoice paid → supply invoice paid
6. Supply invoice paid → car received
7. Transport ordered → transport conducted (~3 days)
8. ZB2 sent to buyer (released only after payment received)

---

### KR4: Reduce number of incidents

Quality was neglected in T1; insufficient testing before releases. Many incidents stemmed from the invoicing complexity (buyback age discount structures, two-invoice flow). Business team was unhappy. Retro held. Goal is to significantly reduce incidents in T2.

---

## KPI Tree (North Star breakdown)

**North Star:** Gross Profit = gross profit per car x cars sold

**Gross profit per car:** ~€500 target (currently running ~€650-700; likely re-forecasting to ~€600)
- Additional services (e.g. transport): charge ~€250, cost ~€170, upside ~€80

**Cars sold** = MAU x bids per MAU per car x conversion rate x available supply

**Health metrics (indirect):**
- Sales-to-opportunity ratio (% of supplier cars FINN actually sells)
- Customer satisfaction score (dealer survey, small sample)
- De-fleeting cycle time
- Dealer touch points (personal + automated)
- Internal tooling efficiency

**Gross profit sources:**
- RAS (primary path)
- Presales (mainly BMW production slots, ~3,500 cars YTD, ~80-90% BMW)
- Total damages (~1-2% of fleet, sold via Copart, Schuster)
