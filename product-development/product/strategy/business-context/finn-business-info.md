## Product Information

### Core Products (Remarketing Domain)

**1. Dealer Product (Buyer Portal)**
B2B used car marketplace for dealerships across Europe. Dealers register, place bids on cars, purchase vehicles, and handle all after-sales transactions: claims, invoice requests, documentation.

**2. Supplier Product (RaaS - Remarketing as a Service)**
New platform pillar (launched Q1 2025) that enables third-party suppliers (OEMs, leasing banks) to sell their own cars through FINN's remarketing platform. Replaces a previously manual process. This is the first step in professionalizing supplier-side operations.

**3. Internal Tooling**
Tools used by the FINN sales team to manage car sales, invoice issuance, and related operational workflows.

### Domain: Remarketing
Mario joined the remarketing department in July 2024. The domain covers the full defleet-to-sale cycle for cars coming off FINN's subscription fleet, plus cars from third-party suppliers (RaaS).

**Key Concept: Defleeting cycle time**
How long it takes a car to move from end-of-lease/defleet to sold. Core operational metric. Reducing this = more revenue, faster capital turnover.
