## Insights & Learnings (running log)

- **Operations fully delegated to DEKRA.** Status updates flow via email, not via UWMS/Lineos integration with FINN. Implication: any portal we build must accommodate DEKRA-as-the-operator pattern.
- **Pricing approval is hard-required.** Christoph escalates to Finance Director for large RV deviations — automation must surface fast approvals, not bypass them.
- **Visibility gap on blocked vehicles** caused a real lost-sale incident: cars blocked for FINN for weeks that FINN no longer wanted, with no path for Nissan to sell elsewhere.

## Open Risks / Blockers

- Lean team capacity — any integration that adds OEM-side steps will be quietly dropped.
