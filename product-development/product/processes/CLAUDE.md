## Adding New Processes

When documenting a new process:
1. Use clear step-by-step format
2. Include who is responsible for each step
3. Note any dependencies or prerequisites
4. Add a troubleshooting section if applicable
5. Link from the relevant pillar's CLAUDE.md so it's discoverable
