## Working Style
- Values honesty and constructive challenge — explicitly asked for direct, critical feedback, no sugarcoating
- Leads with rationale: presents designs with context on WHY, then invites challenge
- Prefers meeting-based feedback over async Figma comments for nuanced discussions
- Practical about usability: "Design is not about how it looks — if usability is fixed, I'm happy"
- Designs full vision first, then lets PM + engineers decide phasing

## How to Work Well with Iryna
- Be direct. She won't be offended by critical comments.
- Challenge designs — she expects and welcomes it
- Don't restrict her to existing component library upfront
- Give her context on customer behavior and business logic; she turns that into UX decisions
- Set up meetings for any real design discussion (not just Figma async)
