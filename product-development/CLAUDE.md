## Pillars

The Remarketing domain breaks into three product/work pillars. Most folders below use these as subfolder names:

| Pillar | What's in it |
|--------|-------------|
| `supplier/` | RaaS supplier product (selling FINN remarketing-as-a-service to OEMs/leasing partners) |
| `buyer/` | B2B buyer/dealer product (auction/sales platform for car dealerships and trade buyers) |
| `operations/` | Both compound operations (tyre change, deregistration, refurbishment) and customer operations (PoA, transport coordination, paper sending) — tracked together |
